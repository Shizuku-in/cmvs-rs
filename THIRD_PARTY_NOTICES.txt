Used for PS2A byte transform and LZSS format research/implementation.
Also used for CPZ6 header layout and XOR field decoding.
CPZ6 index/resource transforms and the customized CMVS digest were ported
to bounded Rust implementations from CPZV6.cpp and cmvs_md5.cpp.
PB3B header transforms, type-1 block layout and split-stream LZSS were
reimplemented using Pb3Decoder/Pb3Decoder/PB3B.cpp and CPB.cpp as format
references, with bounded validation and current-binary/Python cross-checks.
